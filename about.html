About Ambassador Loving Care

A Georgia home health agency built on skilled clinical care, respect, and the belief that people heal best at home.

Our mission

To be a trusted ambassador of care in your home

Ambassador Loving Care Inc. provides skilled nursing and therapeutic services so patients can recover from illness or injury without leaving the place they know best. We treat every home as a clinical setting and every family as a partner.

We are a home health agency (NPI 1467107482) based in Powder Springs, Georgia. Our work is physician-directed, nurse-supervised, and centered on safety, dignity, and independence.

Administrator / Registered Nurse

Patience N. Ogbuefi, RN

Patience is a registered nurse licensed in Georgia (RN155786) and the administrator of Ambassador Loving Care. She oversees clinical quality, caregiver standards, and the coordination of care with referring physicians.

Her focus is straightforward: send prepared clinicians into the home, listen to families, and keep the plan of care aligned with what the doctor ordered and what the patient actually needs.
