Our belief

True healing happens where you’re most comfortable—at home

Ambassador Loving Care delivers personalized home health for individuals and families across Powder Springs, Cobb County, and surrounding metro Atlanta communities.

Our nurses, therapists, and home health aides work closely with you, your family, and your physician to support a safer recovery, protect independence, and help you avoid unnecessary hospital visits. Most medically ordered services may be covered by Medicare, Medicaid, and major insurance plans.

Why families stay with us

The work happens in your house, not ours

Your rooms, your routines
Recovery goes better when you can sleep in your own bed and keep the life you already have—neighbors, church, the chair by the window.
The same people, week after week
We do not rotate a new face through the door every visit. Nurses and aides learn how you take medications, what hurts, and who to call.
Help that aims at independence
The point is not to take over. It is to get you dressing, walking, and managing a day with as little extra help as you need.
An RN runs the agency
Patience Ogbuefi, RN, administers Ambassador Loving Care. Your physician’s order is the plan we follow, and we keep that doctor in the loop.

If you need us

Start with a phone call. We take it from there.

  1. Someone reaches out

    You, a family member, a hospital case manager, or a physician can call (678) 643-1188. Skilled care needs a doctor’s order.

  2. We look at coverage

    We review the order and help confirm what Medicare, Medicaid, or your insurance may pay before anyone comes to the house.

  3. A nurse visits

    On the first visit we go through medications, safety in the home, and what you want to be able to do again.

  4. The schedule begins

    Nurses, therapists, or aides come as often as the doctor ordered, on days that fit how you actually live.
