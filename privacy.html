Privacy & notices

Ambassador Loving Care Inc. respects the privacy of patients, families, and website visitors. Information you share by phone or through this website is used to respond to your request for care, coordinate services, and meet our legal and clinical obligations.

Do not send sensitive medical records through the website form. Call (678) 643-1188 for guidance on securely sharing protected health information.

Notice of non-discrimination

Ambassador Loving Care Inc. does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, sex, or any other status protected by applicable law. We provide language assistance and accessible communication upon request when reasonably possible.
